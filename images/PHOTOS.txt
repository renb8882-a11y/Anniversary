Add your photos to this folder using these exact filenames:

  bg.jpg       - Login page background (romantic photo of you two)
  month-1.jpg  - Photo from Month 1 together
  month-2.jpg  - Photo from Month 2 together
  month-4.jpg  - Photo from Month 4 together
  month-6.jpg  - Photo from Month 6 together
  month-12.jpg - Photo from your 1-Year Anniversary

JPG, PNG, and WEBP all work — just rename the file to match exactly.
If a photo is missing, a beautiful placeholder will show instead.
